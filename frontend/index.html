How the search ran

How TripWise decides

  1. FilterDestinations that break a hard rule (too short a trip, wrong type, too close to home) are removed first.
  2. SearchBreadth-first search builds trips step by step: destination, transport, stay, activities. Branches over budget are dropped immediately.
  3. ScoreEach trip earns points for interest match, category, budget fit, trip length and transport preference.
  4. RankThe best trip per destination is kept and the top five are shown with the reasons.

TripWise uses illustrative estimates, not live fares or prices. Destination photos load from Wikipedia and are shown under their own licences.
